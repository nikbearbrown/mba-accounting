# Principles of Accounting
## Compact Tik TOC Architecture

**Book type:** MBA / undergraduate business course textbook  
**Status:** Retrospective architecture for developed content  

## Core Thesis

This book teaches accounting as the language of business decisions: how transactions become statements, how statements become insight, and how managers use accounting information responsibly.

## Primary Reader

MBA students, business undergraduates, founders, and professionals who need accounting literacy without becoming accountants.

## Learning Arc

1. **Financial accounting foundations:** transactions, accruals, statements, and controls.
2. **Analysis and interpretation:** ratios, cash flow, revenue, inventory, assets, liabilities, and equity.
3. **Managerial use:** cost behavior, budgeting, variance, decision-making, and ethical reporting.

## Chapter Architecture

Each chapter should start with a business decision and show what accounting information makes that decision possible.

## Terminal Capability

The reader can read core financial statements, interpret accounting signals, and ask better questions about business performance.

## Adoption Risks

- Too much mechanics can obscure managerial meaning.
- AI-generated calculations require checking.
- Ethics must be integrated, not saved for one chapter.
